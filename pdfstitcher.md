# Privacy Policy for PDF Stitch Offline Lite

**Effective Date:** February 12, 2026  
**Last Updated:** February 12, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how PDF Stitch Offline Lite ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

PDF Stitch Offline Lite is designed to work completely offline on your device. The app does not:
- Collect personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Track your usage or behavior
- Use analytics or tracking services
- Connect to the internet
- Store data on external servers
- Share information with third parties

## Data Access and Usage

### PDF Files
The app accesses PDF files that you explicitly select through the file picker. These files are:
- Only accessed when you choose them
- Processed entirely on your device
- Never uploaded or transmitted to any server
- Only used for the purpose of merging/stitching PDFs

### Local Storage
The app creates merged PDF files and temporarily stores them on your device. These files:
- Are saved to your device's local storage
- Remain under your complete control
- Can be deleted by you at any time
- Are not accessible to us or any third party

## Permissions

The app requires the following permissions to function:

### Storage Access
- **Why:** To allow you to select PDF files and save merged PDFs
- **How it's used:** Only to read selected PDFs and write the output file
- **Data retention:** Files remain on your device; we don't access them outside of your explicit actions

### File System Access
- **Why:** To open and share the merged PDF files
- **How it's used:** Only when you choose to open or share a merged PDF
- **Data retention:** No data is retained by the app

## Data Retention

We do not retain any data because:
- All processing happens locally on your device
- We do not have access to your files
- No data is transmitted to our servers (we have no servers)

## Third-Party Services

PDF Stitch Offline Lite does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools. The app operates completely independently without any external connections.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect any personal information, it is safe for users of all ages.

## Data Security

Your data security is ensured because:
- All operations are performed locally on your device
- No data is transmitted over the internet
- Your PDF files never leave your device through our app (unless you explicitly share them)
- We have no access to your data

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

## Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete from our side. You have complete control over all files on your device and can delete them at any time through your device's file manager.
