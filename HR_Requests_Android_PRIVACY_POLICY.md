# Privacy Policy for HR Requests (Android)

_Last updated: May 28, 2025_

Thank you for using HR Requests (“the App”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Android app.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App does **not** collect personal information directly from users. There are no sign-up forms, and no personal details such as name, email, or job title are collected via the app interface.

### File Attachments

When submitting HR requests (such as for loans or vacations), you may attach files (images, PDFs) using the file picker. These files are securely uploaded to our backend using Firebase. Files are not shared with any third parties.

## 2. Use of Information

We use the files you provide exclusively to process your HR requests. Files are stored securely and are only accessible by authorized personnel within your organization.

## 3. Notifications

The App may send personalized push notifications to inform you of the status of your requests and to provide reminders. You can control notification preferences via your device settings.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** share any of your files or information with third parties.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

This App is intended only for authorized employees. It is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion

Users cannot request the deletion of data or files submitted via the App. The App is designed for corporate use, and user accounts cannot be created or deleted by end users.

## 7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your files and information using secure transmission and storage via Firebase.
