# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 13, 2026**

## PDF Rearranger

This Privacy Policy describes how PDF Rearranger ("we", "us", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

PDF Rearranger is a fully offline application. All PDF processing occurs entirely on your device. We do not have servers that receive your data.

## File Access

The App requests access to your device's storage solely to:

- Open PDF files you select
- Save modified PDF files to your chosen location

Your PDF files are never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any external server. All file operations happen locally on your device.

## Data Storage

The App stores only your preferences (such as theme and language settings) locally on your device using standard platform storage. This data never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App does not collect any user data, it is safe for users of all ages.

## Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

- **Storage/File Access**: Required to open and save PDF files on your device.

These permissions are used solely for the functionality described above.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
