# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: February 14, 2026**

## Smart Sebha — Digital Tasbeeh & Adhkar

This Privacy Policy describes how Smart Sebha ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information.**

Smart Sebha operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics.

### 2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data **locally on your device only** to provide its core functionality:

- **Tasbeeh counts and presets** — your dhikr counter values and custom presets
- **App preferences** — your chosen theme (light/dark) and language (Arabic/English)

This data is stored using local storage mechanisms (Hive database and SharedPreferences) and **never leaves your device**. No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.

### 3. Internet Access

Smart Sebha does **not** require or use internet access. The app functions entirely offline, including all fonts, audio, and adhkar content, which are bundled within the app.

### 4. Third-Party Services

Smart Sebha does **not** integrate any third-party services, including but not limited to:

- Analytics or tracking tools
- Advertising networks
- Cloud storage or backup services
- Social media SDKs
- Crash reporting services

### 5. Children's Privacy

Smart Sebha does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for use by users of all ages.

### 6. Permissions

Smart Sebha does not request any device permissions (such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, or internet access).

### 7. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by:

- Using the **Reset All Data** option in the app's Settings tab
- Clearing the app's data through your device settings
- Uninstalling the app

### 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
