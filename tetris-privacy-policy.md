Privacy Policy for Neon Tetris

Last Updated: 12 December 2025

Neon Tetris (“the Game”) is developed and published by Ahmed Mostafa.
This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Game.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, share, or process any personal information from users.

Specifically, we do not:

Collect names, emails, phone numbers, or any personal identifiers

Track location or device data

Collect gameplay data, analytics, or usage statistics

Use cookies or similar tracking technologies

Integrate with third-party analytics or advertising tools

The Game is designed to be fully functional without requiring any personal data.

2. Permissions

Neon Tetris does not request any special permissions on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game does not use:

Third-party SDKs

Analytics tools

Advertising networks

Social media integrations

No data is shared with any third parties because no data is collected.

4. Children’s Privacy

Neon Tetris does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.
Since no data is collected from any user, the Game is safe for all ages.

5. Security

Since no personal information is collected or stored, there is no risk of unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure of your data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game’s functionality changes.
Any future revisions will be published on this page.
